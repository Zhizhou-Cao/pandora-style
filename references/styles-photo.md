# Photography and Darkroom Styles

Keep these effects photographic inside masks. Do not convert the entire image unless explicitly requested.

| Slug | Fit | Regional visual contract | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gelatin-silver` | both | Neutral black-and-white, clear silver grain, firm blacks, smooth midtone separation, fiber paper. | sepia unless requested, plastic smoothing |
| `platinum-print` | semantic | Matte silvery-gray tonal range, rich soft blacks, long quiet midtones, plain paper surface. | hard clipping, saturated color |
| `salt-print` | semantic | Warm brown-violet image embedded in matte paper fibers, soft detail and edge falloff. | crisp modern sharpness, glossy finish |
| `albumen-print` | semantic | Warm sepia/purple-black, delicate highlight detail, faint yellowed binder sheen and aged edges. | heavy scratches, fake antique frame |
| `photogram` | semantic | Object-contact silhouette, white-to-gray shadow trace on dark sensitized field, ambiguous translucency. | conventional camera perspective inside mask |
| `solarization` | both | Partial tonal reversal with narrow bright or dark contour bands around high-contrast edges. | global negative, neon outline |
| `chemigram` | semantic | Chemical pools, resist islands, branching stains and chance edges attached to selected contours. | generic watercolor, unrelated splatter |
| `luminogram` | geometry | Soft light-written gradients and luminous bands without literal object detail, aligned to source direction. | lens flare stickers, rainbow glow |
| `gum-bichromate` | semantic | Pigment-rich brushed photographic tone, selectively washed detail, painterly contrast and paper tooth. | uniform photo filter, glossy color |
| `infrared-false-color` | semantic | Process-native false color: bright foliage, dark sky or surreal channel mapping while geometry remains photographic. | arbitrary rainbow, altered scene layout |
| `cross-processed-film` | both | Shifted cyan/green shadows and warm highlights, dense contrast, film grain, restrained chemical cast. | HDR, uniform Instagram filter |
| `bleach-bypass` | both | Retained silver, desaturated color, strong grain, deep blacks and metallic highlights. | oversaturated color, cinematic relighting |
| `instant-film` | geometry | Soft color, modest dynamic range, chemical edge variation and small undeveloped patches inside region only. | white Polaroid frame unless source-derived |
| `scannergram` | semantic | Flatbed sharp contact plane with rapid depth falloff, streaked movement and dark peripheral depth. | normal camera depth, global black background |
| `double-exposure` | semantic | Two source-derived layers with controlled transparency, shared contour or directional relationship. | unrelated stock imagery, muddy opacity |
| `motion-smear` | geometry | Directional dragged exposure following source movement or perspective, with one stable anchor edge. | random blur, whole-image camera shake |
| `selective-color` | both | Near-monochrome local photograph retaining exactly one meaningful source color. | arbitrary painted color, multiple accents |
| `thermal-imaging` | semantic | Process-native heat-map palette mapped to semantic mass and value, with crisp thermal boundaries. | decorative rainbow, fake UI labels |
