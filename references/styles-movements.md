# Art and Design Movement Styles

Use movement-level visual grammar without imitating a named living artist. Preserve source identity and avoid copying signature artworks.

| Slug | Fit | Regional visual contract | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bauhaus` | geometry | Functional primary geometry, asymmetric balance, circles/bars derived from source axes, restrained sans type. | generic logo composition, excessive decoration |
| `constructivist` | geometry | Strong diagonals, red/black/cream planes, cropped photo fragments, forceful directional typography. | propaganda symbols, unrelated slogans |
| `suprematist` | geometry | Sparse floating geometric planes, high negative space, limited primary/neutral palette, source-axis tension. | dense collage, literal object tracing |
| `de-stijl` | geometry | Orthogonal black lines, rectangular planes, primary color fields mapped to architectural divisions. | diagonals unless source demands, gradients |
| `swiss-international` | geometry | Rational grid fragments, asymmetric sans typography, disciplined white space and precise photo crops. | corporate logo, full-page grid leakage |
| `brutalist-editorial` | geometry | Raw high-contrast type, exposed alignment, stark black/white blocks and deliberately blunt cropping. | polished luxury design, random clutter |
| `dada-photomontage` | both | Abrupt scale shifts, newspaper fragments, cut photography, chance alignment and disruptive small type. | coherent ad layout, too many unrelated objects |
| `surrealist-collage` | semantic | One uncanny source-derived substitution or impossible overlap, quiet dream space and material seams. | fantasy scenery overload, named-artist imitation |
| `cubist-facets` | geometry | Angular planes, compressed depth, limited viewpoints, overlapping source fragments and muted structural color. | total scene fragmentation, kaleidoscope symmetry |
| `futurist-dynamism` | geometry | Repeated directional forms, speed lines, fragmented motion and converging urban energy. | comic action effects, unrelated machinery |
| `pop-art-comic` | both | Bold contour, Ben-Day-like dots, flat high-chroma fields and selective dramatic crop. | trademark characters, speech-bubble clutter |
| `op-art` | geometry | Black-white line modulation or repeated geometry producing controlled optical vibration within masks. | seizure-like full-frame patterns, color overload |
| `minimalism` | geometry | Serial or singular simple form, exact spacing, restrained material and large internal quiet. | decorative texture, arbitrary accents |
| `color-field` | semantic | Broad low-detail color areas mapped to atmosphere or shadow, soft boundaries and minimal focal interruption. | many small marks, hard illustration detail |
| `geometric-abstraction` | geometry | Hard-edge source-derived line, plane and color relationships without literal surface detail. | arbitrary shapes disconnected from scene |
| `memphis-postmodern` | geometry | Playful elementary forms, black-white pattern and vivid contrasting color, localized energetic imbalance. | covering every surface, uncontrolled palette |
| `art-deco` | geometry | Stepped symmetry, streamlined rays, metallic/black geometry and elegant architectural rhythm. | ornate Victorian filigree, global frame |
| `art-nouveau` | semantic | Organic whiplash contour, plant-like flowing line and flat decorative color following real forms. | generic floral border, excessive ornament |
