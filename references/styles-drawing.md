# Painting and Drawing Styles

Keep marks visibly hand-made and source-derived. Choose one primary mark system and at most one support texture.

| Slug | Fit | Regional visual contract | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ink-wash` | semantic | Black/gray wash masses, dry-brush contour, bleeding water edges and generous untouched paper. | detailed contour everywhere, extra colors |
| `watercolor-bloom` | semantic | Transparent layered washes, cauliflower blooms, granulation and pigment pooling along real contours. | opaque digital gradients, hard vector edges |
| `gouache-flat` | both | Opaque matte color planes, simplified shape blocks, slight brush drag and minimal modeling. | glossy paint, airbrushed realism |
| `charcoal` | semantic | Powdery black masses, erased highlights, rubbed transitions, forceful gestural contour and paper tooth. | clean ink line, smooth grayscale filter |
| `graphite-hatching` | both | Pencil-value hatching, pressure variation, construction traces and selective smudging. | uniform machine hatch, photoreal pencil render |
| `colored-pencil` | semantic | Layered directional strokes, visible paper grain, restrained source-resonant colors and incomplete fill. | waxy full coverage, childlike outline |
| `oil-pastel` | semantic | Dense broken color, waxy drag, scumbled overlaps and blunt source shapes. | smooth vector fill, rainbow palette |
| `chalk-pastel` | semantic | Powdery soft edges, velvety color masses, finger-blended transitions and exposed ground. | crisp glossy shapes, heavy outlines |
| `dry-brush` | both | Bristle-separated strokes, skipped paper texture, directional black or one-color marks following form. | wet watercolor blooms, uniform opacity |
| `palette-knife-oil` | geometry | Broad impasto-like planar strokes, scraped edges and compressed color blocks; keep depth visually flat. | thick 3D relief shadows, tiny brush detail |
| `fresco` | both | Mineral matte pigment, plaster grain, faded flat color and small cracks integrated with form. | fake ruined-wall frame, glossy paint |
| `pointillism` | semantic | Separated source-color dots that optically mix, density controlled by local value and contour. | uniform halftone screen, allover dot carpet |
| `expressionist-brush` | semantic | Distorted but recognizable form, emotionally weighted strokes and high-chroma source-responsive color. | named-artist imitation, complete scene distortion |
| `contour-line` | both | One or few interrupted lines preserving silhouette, direction and overlap, mostly blank interior. | coloring-book closure, equal line weight |
| `technical-drawing` | geometry | Precise sectional line, measured construction marks, cutaway fragments and sparse annotations if requested. | fake dense metadata, blueprint blue by default |
| `architectural-render` | geometry | Pencil/ink structure with limited watercolor plane, perspective retained and minor detail omitted. | full building redesign, glossy 3D render |
| `botanical-engraving` | semantic | Fine descriptive contour and selective cross-hatching for organic subjects, aged print character. | leaf-by-leaf density outside selected subject |
| `comic-ink` | both | Bold varied ink contour, selective black spotting, speed-aware hatching and restrained flat color. | copyrighted characters, speech bubbles by default |
