# Material and Craft Styles

Translate the selected photograph into a plausible flat material surface. Preserve source contours and avoid artificial mockup depth.

| Slug | Fit | Regional visual contract | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| `torn-paper-collage` | both | Printed source fragments on mismatched paper stocks, visible irregular fibers and flat overlapping seams. | drop shadows, curled corners, scrapbook clutter |
| `papier-colle` | geometry | Flat cut paper, newspaper/type texture and painted planes integrated into source geometry. | random magazine imagery, excessive layers |
| `patchwork-quilt` | geometry | Source planes translated into unequal fabric pieces, subtle seams and restrained stitched joins. | uniform checkerboard, puffy 3D quilting |
| `boro-sashiko` | both | Indigo or source-resonant worn cloth patches, visible repair seams and irregular running stitches. | pristine luxury textile, perfect repetition |
| `embroidery` | semantic | Directional thread stitches following object contour, limited thread palette and visible base cloth. | fully covering every pixel, plastic thread |
| `cross-stitch` | geometry | Counted X-stitch grid locally resolving the subject, limited palette and visible even-weave ground. | global pixel grid, photoreal thread density |
| `tapestry` | semantic | Simplified woven color masses, warp/weft texture and softened stepped contours. | furry carpet texture, excessive thread detail |
| `batik` | semantic | Wax-resist cracks, flat dyed shapes, dark contour and source-derived organic pattern breaks. | generic tropical motifs, rainbow dye |
| `stained-glass` | both | Colored glass cells divided by dark lead cames, source-value translucency and a limited luminous palette. | glossy 3D bevels, random mosaic cells |
| `mosaic` | both | Unequal tesserae following form and value, visible grout, simplified tonal grouping. | uniform bathroom tiles, photoreal micro-mosaic |
| `ceramic-sgraffito` | semantic | Matte clay/slip field with scratched lines revealing contrasting body beneath; restrained earthen palette. | glossy porcelain render, arbitrary cracks |
| `ceramic-transfer` | geometry | Slightly imperfect printed source motif transferred onto a glazed-looking but visually flat ceramic field. | 3D plate mockup, decorative border |
| `marbled-paper` | semantic | Controlled combed or stone-like pigment veins guided by source flow, one dominant color family. | random full-frame marbling, psychedelic rainbow |
| `frottage` | both | Graphite/crayon rubbings that reveal source-aligned surface relief and broken raised textures. | photographic texture overlay, uniform noise |
| `cut-paper` | both | Two to four clean but hand-cut flat paper shapes, slight fiber roughness and strong negative space. | vector-perfect clipping, layered shadows |
| `cloisonne` | semantic | Dark wire-like cells, flat enamel colors and simplified contour compartments. | jewel-like 3D glare, excessive ornament |
| `terrazzo` | geometry | Source plane becomes mineral aggregate chips in a restrained ground, density tracking local value. | confetti decoration, many unrelated colors |
| `woven-rattan` | geometry | Interlaced strips follow structural direction and perspective, with simplified source tonal bands. | photoreal basket object, uniform global weave |
